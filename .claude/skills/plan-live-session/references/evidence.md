# Evidence: the benchmarks this skill writes against

These are **correlations observed on other channels**, not laws, and not our numbers. Apply them when
writing titles, opening lines, run-of-shows and Shorts. Once `docs/live-sessions/history.json` holds
**ten sessions with real `results`** (views, watch time, attendance, clips used), prefer our own
numbers and say so in the blueprint's assumptions.

None of these benchmarks may appear on a slide or in public copy as a fact. They steer the writing;
they are not claims. If one is ever quoted publicly it must go through the facts table with a
fetched source like any other claim.

| # | Benchmark | How the skill applies it | Source | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| E1 | Titles of about five words and 30 characters or fewer drew about **60% more views** | Titles ≤ 34 characters, aim for five words; the Idea rubric's `promise` line | 1of10 study of 300,000 YouTube videos | As reported by Ali; study URL not yet recorded here |
| E2 | Text-heavy thumbnails drew about **19% fewer views** | Thumbnail text ≤ 10 characters | 1of10 study of 300,000 YouTube videos | As reported by Ali; study URL not yet recorded here |
| E3 | Negative or contrarian framing drew about **22% more views** | The `tension` line; the contrarian-take lens; contrarian sound bite required | 1of10 study of 300,000 YouTube videos | As reported by Ali; study URL not yet recorded here |
| E4 | Opening lines on million-view videos: median **12 words**; about a third say "you"; **2%** open with a greeting; about **1 in 400** ask for a subscribe | Opening line ~12 words, addresses "you", never a greeting, never a subscribe ask | OverseerOS analysis of 4,148 hooks | As reported by Ali; URL not yet recorded here |
| E5 | Educational long-form performs best at **18–24 minutes** | The edited avatar video targets 18–24 minutes | Program working benchmark | Unsourced heuristic |
| E6 | Shorts perform best at **40–55 seconds**, opening on a direct statement | Shorts are cut from sound bites that make sense with zero context | Program working benchmark | Unsourced heuristic |
| E7 | News is "breaking" for **2 days**, an explainer for **14**, a proof point up to **90**, background after | News-age ceilings in the Idea rubric's `news` line and in topic scoring | Program working benchmark | Unsourced heuristic |
| E8 | Live sessions: strongest material in the **first five minutes**, a new segment every **8–10 minutes**, a chat prompt in **every** segment, finished within **90 minutes** where possible | Run-of-show rules; the Room scorecard | Program working benchmark, consistent with `build-class-deck` §4 (Weeks 4–5 overran on volume) | Partly evidenced in-repo |

**To upgrade a row:** add the study's URL and publication date, change Status to `Sourced`, and note
the date you checked it. Do not change a number without the source in hand.
