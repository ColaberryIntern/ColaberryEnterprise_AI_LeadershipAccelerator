What this website collects, and why.

This notice covers refactored.ai, the public website. The platform you sign in to has its own terms, presented at sign-up.

On this website

Analytics. We record which pages are visited and which calls to action are used, so we can tell which parts of this site are useful. This is first-party and is not sold or shared with advertising networks.

What we do not put in analytics. Whatever you type into a form is not sent to analytics. The event records that a form was started or submitted, not what it said.

The contact form. Name, email, organisation and message, kept so a person can reply to you and so we know where the enquiry came from.

Fonts. Typefaces load from Google Fonts, which means Google receives the request. Everything else on this site is served from our own infrastructure.
