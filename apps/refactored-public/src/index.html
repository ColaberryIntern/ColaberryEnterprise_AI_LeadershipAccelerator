Video Feedback Service (VFS)

Refactored’s integrated Colaberry Artificial Intelligence Video Feedback Service (CAI VFS) combines emotion detection, facial detection, and speech-to-text systems to help develop you into an effective communicator and build up those human skills. Using the multitude of machine learning tools that power our AI, we provide feedback to you on two video metrics and three audio metrics. The video metrics are facial expression and head pose. The audio metrics include the speech rate, voiced ratio, and filler word rate. We also provide you with a ful transciption.

Not only with our Video Feedback Service help improve your human skills, but it will help with interview preparation and presentation skills to get you job-ready when the time comes.

The solutions developed in this competition will reduce training time by at least 50% for occupations with a living wage and will be provided at no cost to the individual.
