AI workforce

A chatbot answers. An employee is accountable.

The distinction is not marketing. It is whether anything constrains what the software may do, and whether a named person answers for it.

Each has a job, a manager, and a list of things it is not allowed to do. The second list matters more than the first.

The accountability loop

  1. A person sets the goal

    Direction comes from leadership, not from whoever wrote the last prompt.

  2. The agent works inside its boundary

    Permissions decide what it can reach. The boundary is enforced by the system.

  3. Consequential decisions come back

    Anything that spends money, contacts a person or changes a commitment waits for approval.

  4. The work is inspectable afterwards

    What was done, by which agent, under whose authority, on what basis.

What this is not

These agents are not autonomous businesses and they are not a replacement for the people who manage them. They do bounded work well and hand back anything consequential. Any vendor describing a system that never needs a person is describing something nobody can be held responsible for.
