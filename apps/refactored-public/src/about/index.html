About

It started as a way to get people into work that pays.

The platform grew out of workforce development, which is why evidence of capability is built into it rather than bolted on.

From training platform to operating platform

The original problem was narrow and hard: take people without a technical background and get them into data careers, provably. That forced two things that most learning software never needs — real project work, and evidence strong enough for a hiring manager to act on.

Those turn out to be the same primitives an organisation needs to put AI agents to work: a record of what was actually done, and a way to decide whether to trust it. The platform grew along that line rather than pivoting.

Today the same software runs learning, customer software delivery, an AI workforce and several distinct brands. Each one exercises a different part of it, which is the point.
