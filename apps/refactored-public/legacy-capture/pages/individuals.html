Why Data?

In this Age of AI and increasing automation in the workplace, data skills are indispensable. Every role across every industry will soon require some level of data fluency. Becoming a data thinker and continuously building those skills will not only future-proof your career but will open up to you a world of new, exciting and rewarding career opportunities.

Why Human Skills?

The demand for human skills - like communication, collaboration, lifelong learning, and problem solving - is already high in the labor market. As rapid technological innovations continue to redefine the workplace, the ability to manage the human side of work will become critical to one's success in the future of work.
