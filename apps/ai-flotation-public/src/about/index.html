About

AI Flotation builds the system your workflow has been standing in for.

The name is the method. In froth flotation you make the valuable material rise and let the waste settle; here the work worth seeing lifts out of manual routine and becomes something you can watch, question and govern.

How we operate

Agents build. People decide.
We are not selling autonomy. The interesting engineering is in where the machine is required to stop.
Evidence beats assertion
Anything we claim about a release should be checkable in your own room. If it is not checkable, we should not have claimed it.
Honest states over reassuring ones
Not measured, not run, stale, waived, failed, approved and released are seven different things and are never collapsed into a tick.
The refusal is the feature
A gate that has never stopped anything has not been tested. We would rather be slowed by our own system than discover it was decorative.

What is deliberately not on this page. Years in business, team size, client counts, certifications and partner badges. When any of those are true and verifiable they will appear here, and not before.
