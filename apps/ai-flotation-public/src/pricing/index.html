The shape of it

Why it is priced this way

Ideas are unlimited. Active builds are not.
Keep as many projects in your backlog as you like. What the membership governs is how many are consuming delivery capacity at once, because that is the thing that is actually scarce.
Every paid build has a named human.
A project manager who is accountable for the work, the exceptions and the release. AI speed, human accountability — not a queue.
We do not sell hours, prompts, tokens or agents.
Those are our costs to manage, not your unit of value. You should not have to think about how many agents ran to get what you asked for.

Start with the work, not the plan

Which membership fits depends on what you are building and how much of it you want moving at once — and neither of us knows that yet. Tell us what the work is first. If there is no system worth building, we will say so.
