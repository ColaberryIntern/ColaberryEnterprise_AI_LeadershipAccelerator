Trust Before Intelligence

Fast software is easy to promise. Software you can trust is harder.

AI Flotation builds around the Architecture of Trust: what AI agents need, the infrastructure that has to exist around them, and the operating evidence that keeps them trustworthy after launch.

INPACT™

What agents need

Six dimensions. In our delivery system these carry a score, and a release can be refused on them — they are not a poster.

Instant
The answer arrives inside the moment the work actually happens.
Natural
A person asks in their own words, not in the system's.
Permitted
The agent may do this, and the boundary is enforced rather than agreed.
Adaptive
It changes as the work changes, instead of aging into wrongness.
Contextual
It knows the situation it is answering into.
Transparent
Why it did that can be shown afterwards, to someone who was not there.

Architecture of Trust

Seven layers, in this order

Each layer depends on the ones before it. Intelligence built on stale data is confident and wrong, which is worse than slow.

01
Multi-Modal Storage
What the organisation actually holds, in the forms it holds it
02
Real-Time Data Fabric
Current, not last night's extract
03
Semantic
What the words mean here, agreed once
04
Intelligence
Retrieval and reasoning over that meaning
05
Governance
What may happen, and who may authorise it
06
Observability
What did happen, visible without asking anyone
07
Orchestration
The work coordinated across all of it

GOALS™

How trust is sustained, once there is something to measure

Governance
Authority is enforced in the system, not remembered by people.
Observability
A failure can be traced from symptom to cause.
Availability
It is there when the work needs it.
Lexicon
The same word means the same thing across the business.
Solid
It behaves the same way tomorrow.

Before a system operates, these are not scored. A project carries them as Required, Planned, Needs decision or Not yet measurable. We do not show operational maturity for something that has not run yet, and a weak dimension is never averaged away by the four beside it.

Where this comes from

The Architecture of Trust is the framework set out in Trust Before Intelligence by Ram Katamaraja. It is the standard our delivery system is built to, which is why the same vocabulary appears in your project room rather than only on this page.

What this is not. Trust Before Intelligence is an engineering and operating standard. It is not a certification, and it is not a claim of regulatory compliance in any jurisdiction.
