Delivery standard

You can watch it happen

Clients get their own room in the system. Not a status email — the actual record: what was decided, what shipped, and the evidence behind it, including the checks that were waived and why.

A worked example. A release ships with six checks passed and one waived. The room shows Passed for the six, and Waived for the seventh with the written reason beside it. It is never rendered as a pass. If nothing measured an outcome, the room says Unknown rather than showing a zero.
