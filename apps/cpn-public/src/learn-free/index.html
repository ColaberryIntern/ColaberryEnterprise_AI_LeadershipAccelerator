Why start before you apply

Beginning is the part you control.

Applications are not open yet, and when they are, they will ask what you have already done. This is the answer to that question.

It shows you what the work is

A scholarship asks for about 25 hours a week for a year. A free account is the cheapest way to find out whether that is a commitment you want to make.

It costs you nothing to stop

If you try it and it is not for you, you have lost no money and owe nobody an explanation. That is a real answer too.

Nothing is gated behind us

We do not stand between you and the learning. There is no queue here, and waiting for OpportunityLift is never the reason you have not started.
