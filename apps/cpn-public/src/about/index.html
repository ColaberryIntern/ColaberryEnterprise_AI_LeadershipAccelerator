About

A small organisation with one job.

Career Pathways Network raises money and turns it into paid for career training for people in Dallas Fort Worth who could not otherwise afford it. OpportunityLift is the name of that scholarship program.

Why this exists

The training that leads to a first job in AI is not secret, and for a lot of people it is not out of reach because it is hard. It is out of reach because it costs money and takes hours that are already spoken for.

We cannot give anyone the hours. We can remove the cost, and we can be straightforward about what the hours are: about 25 a week, for a year. Anything that pretends the commitment is smaller than that would set people up to fail.

Training providers, and a conflict we are not going to hide

A scholarship buys a year of training from an approved provider. Today there is one.

Colaberry Inc. is our approved training provider. The free introductory AI training we point people to, refactored.ai, is also operated by Colaberry.

Colaberry is a related party. Career Pathways Network's president, Ali Muwwakkil, is also Colaberry's managing director, and this organisation's principal office is Colaberry's office. That is a real conflict of interest and it does not go away by being described carefully.

What we can do is be plain about it and constrain it. We are not an independent body that evaluated the market and happened to select Colaberry, and we will not present ourselves that way. A provider agreement, at a rate reviewed against comparable programs and approved by the board members who have no interest in Colaberry, is work still to be done. No provider has been paid anything, because no scholarship has been awarded yet.

A scholarship is defined as a year of approved training, not a year at one named school. Colaberry has no exclusivity and no guaranteed number of scholars.

What we have not done yet

We have not filed for federal tax exempt status. Contributions are not tax deductible at this time.

We have not awarded a scholarship, signed a provider agreement, or paid a provider.

We have not opened applications, and we have not finished setting eligibility or the selection process.

We are not processing payments on this site, and we cannot yet send automatic email from this domain.

This page will be updated as each of those changes. If you want to know when, register your interest and we will tell you.
