What it is

A scholarship, not a loan.

Twelve months of training at an approved provider, paid for by OpportunityLift. There is nothing to pay back. What it asks from you is time.

Twelve months, fully funded

We do not award a scholarship until the whole year is paid for. That way a scholar is never told halfway through that the money ran out.

About 25 hours a week

Training, projects and career preparation. This is the part we cannot do for you, and it is the honest reason the program is not right for everyone.

Work you can show

The pathway is built around projects and a portfolio, so that at the end you have something to point at rather than only a certificate.

We check before we pay

We pay the provider monthly, after the month, and only once we have confirmed the training was there and the scholar took part.

Where the money goes

2,500 dollars, accounted for.

Most of it buys training. The rest pays for running the program honestly, and we publish the whole breakdown rather than a summary of it.

A fully funded scholarship is 2,500 dollars. 1,800 of that pays the training provider at 150 dollars a month for twelve months. The rest covers running the scholarship, a small fund for scholar needs such as exam fees or internet access, and payment fees.

Career Pathways Network holds and controls the money throughout. A donation is never an account belonging to a named person, and money that is not used stays in the scholarship program.

Who runs this

A board of four, and one conflict we will not hide.

Colaberry is our training provider, and that is not an arm's length relationship.

Our president is also Colaberry's managing director, and our office is Colaberry's office. You should know that before you read anything else we say about providers. We are not an independent body that evaluated the market and happened to select Colaberry, and we will not present ourselves as one.
