Where this program is today

Career Pathways Network is a Texas nonprofit corporation, formed in February 2026. It does not yet have federal tax exempt status, so contributions are not tax deductible at this time.

Applications are not open yet. We are still setting who is eligible, how many scholarships we can fund, and how we will choose. You can tell us you are interested now, and we will come back to you when there is something to apply for.

We cannot send automatic email from this site yet. If you fill in a form, a person reads it and follows up. Nothing lands in your inbox on its own.

What a scholarship is

A scholarship is twelve months of training at an approved provider, paid for by OpportunityLift. It is not a loan and there is nothing to pay back. What it asks from you is time: about 25 hours a week, every week, for a year.

Twelve months, fully funded

We do not award a scholarship until the whole year is paid for. That way a scholar is never told halfway through that the money ran out.

About 25 hours a week

Training, projects and career preparation. This is the part we cannot do for you, and it is the honest reason the program is not right for everyone.

Work you can show

The pathway is built around projects and a portfolio, so that at the end you have something to point at rather than only a certificate.

We check before we pay

We pay the provider monthly, after the month, and only once we have confirmed the training was there and the scholar took part.

How it works

1

Start learning for free

You do not need us to begin. The free introductory AI training is provided by refactored.ai, which is operated by Colaberry Inc.

2

Tell us you are interested

Leave your details. When eligibility and dates are set, we come back to you with what to do next.

3

We fund a scholarship

Every 2,500 dollars raised and settled funds one more scholarship. We do not award against money we only expect to receive.

4

You train for a year

Twelve months with an approved provider, with the cost paid month by month as the training is delivered.

Where the money goes

A fully funded scholarship is 2,500 dollars. Most of that, 1,800 dollars, pays the training provider at 150 dollars a month for twelve months. The rest covers running the scholarship, a small fund for scholar needs such as exam fees or internet access, and payment fees.

Career Pathways Network holds and controls the money throughout. A donation is never an account belonging to a named person, and money that is not used stays in the scholarship program.

Who runs this

Career Pathways Network is run by a board of four. Colaberry is our training provider, and that is not an arm's length relationship: our president is also Colaberry's managing director, and our office is Colaberry's office. You should know that before you read anything else we say about providers.
