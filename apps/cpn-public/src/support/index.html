2,500 dollars is one person's year.

That is the whole cost of a scholarship, and 1,800 of it goes straight to training. We are not able to take payments through this site yet, so this page is where you tell us you want to help and a person picks it up.

Read this before you decide

Career Pathways Network is a Texas nonprofit corporation. It does not yet have federal tax exempt status. Contributions are not tax deductible at this time. If deductibility matters to your giving, please wait until that is resolved. We will say so here when it changes.

We are not processing payments on this site. There is no checkout, and nothing here will ask you for card details. Leave your details and a person will follow up about how to give.

We cannot send automatic email from this domain yet, so you will not get a confirmation message. A person will reply.

What your support pays for

A scholarship is not a grant handed over and forgotten. The money is released month by month against training that we have checked actually happened.

1,800 dollars of training

Twelve months with an approved provider, paid at 150 dollars a month after each month is delivered and verified.

200 dollars of scholar support

A reserve for approved pathway costs such as an exam fee, internet access or accessibility support. Requested and approved case by case.

375 dollars of administration

Reviewing applications, checking on scholars during the year, reporting back to you, and the bookkeeping that keeps all of it auditable.

125 dollars of reserve

Payment fees and refunds. Anything unused stays in the scholarship program rather than becoming a surplus somewhere else.

What we will not do with your gift

We will not hand it to a named individual. A contribution supports the scholarship program. If a particular person's story is what moved you, we are glad, and it still does not become their money or their account.

We will not let it choose a scholar. Giving does not influence who is selected, in either direction, and it does not put anyone at the front of a queue.

We will not pass it automatically to a training provider. Providers invoice monthly for training delivered, an authorised person approves each payment, and Colaberry has no standing claim on these funds despite being our current provider and a related party.
