Privacy

What we collect, and what we do with it.

This describes what this website actually does today. It is written to be read rather than agreed to.

What you give us on purpose

The three forms on this site ask for an email address, and optionally a name, phone number, city and state, organisation, role and a message. Only the email is required.

We use it to reply to you, and to tell you when there is something to apply for or to act on. A person reads what you send. We will not sell it, rent it, or pass it to anyone else for their own marketing.

What we collect while you read

This site records how it is used, so we can tell which pages help people and which ones do not. On each page view it records:

  • The page address and title, and the site you came from.
  • Campaign tags in the address, when a link has them.
  • Which links and buttons are pressed.
  • How far down a page you scroll, and roughly how long you stay.
  • When you start filling in a form, and when a submission succeeds.

To recognise the same browser across pages, we store a random identifier in your browser. It is a random string. It is not your name, your email or your location, and it is not bought from or shared with anyone.

If your browser sends a Do Not Track signal, this site records nothing. No page views, no clicks, no identifier. That is checked before anything else happens.

Who else sees it

Career Pathways Network runs this site on shared technology operated by Colaberry Inc., which is also our training provider and a related party. That means Colaberry's systems hold this data on our behalf.

If you become a scholar, the provider delivering your training receives what it needs to teach and support you. It does not receive other applicants' details, selection notes, or anything about who gave money.

Fonts on this site are loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google to fetch them.

What we will not publish

Nothing about an applicant or a scholar goes on a public page without that person agreeing to it first, specifically, and being able to change their mind.

We will not publish application scores, financial circumstances, identity documents, immigration status, health or accommodation information, private messages, or internal notes about a decision. Not with consent either. Those are not ours to make public.

Asking us to delete it

Email {{brand.supportEmail}} and ask. A person will reply. You can also ask us what we hold about you, or ask us to stop contacting you, and we will act on it.
