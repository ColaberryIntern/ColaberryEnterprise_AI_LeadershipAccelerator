Scholarships

Twelve months of training, and nothing to pay back.

A scholarship covers a full year of AI career training at an approved provider. You bring about 25 hours a week. We bring the money and we check, every month, that the training is actually there.

Applications are not open yet

We have not finished setting who is eligible, how many scholarships we can fund in the first year, when applications open, or how we will choose between people. We would rather say that plainly than publish rules we are about to change.

Register your interest below and you will be among the first people we contact when there is something real to apply for.

What the scholarship covers

OpportunityLift pays an approved training provider 150 dollars a month for twelve months, which is 1,800 dollars of training per scholar. You pay nothing, and there is no loan.

  • Twelve months of AI career training with an approved provider.
  • Project work and a portfolio, so there is something to show at the end.
  • Career preparation as part of the pathway.
  • A small scholar support fund, held by OpportunityLift, for approved pathway costs such as an exam fee, internet access or accessibility support. It is requested and approved case by case. It is not a cash payment.

Exactly what is taught, and on what schedule, is set by the provider rather than by us. Our commitment is the funding and the verification.

What it asks of you

About 25 hours a week, every week, for a year. That is the part we cannot do for you, and it is the reason this program is not right for everyone at every point in their life.

If your weeks cannot hold 25 hours right now, the free introductory training at refactored.ai is open to anyone and costs nothing. Starting there is a real answer, not a consolation prize.

How we will choose, and how we will not

The eligibility rules are not written yet. Some things about the decision are already settled, and they will not change with whatever the rules turn out to be.

We make the decision

Career Pathways Network selects every scholar and controls every award. A training provider does not choose who gets funded, and neither does a church, employer or community group that raised money.

Donations do not buy a place

Nobody moves up because they raised money, brought in donors, or was recommended by a large giver. The same rules apply to everyone.

Attention is not a qualification

Views, shares, followers and how compelling a story reads have no effect on whether an application succeeds.

The rules will be published

When the board approves eligibility and the selection process, they go on this page before applications open, not after.

Two things to know before you apply

A scholarship may be taxable income to you. The training providers we fund run workforce programs rather than degree programs, and awards like these are often treated as taxable. We are not able to give tax advice, so please speak to a tax professional about your own situation.

Colaberry is a related party. Colaberry is currently our only approved training provider. Career Pathways Network's president is also Colaberry's managing director, and our office is Colaberry's office. We are not an independent reviewer that happens to recommend Colaberry, and we are not going to describe ourselves as one.
