Partners

Raise a scholarship for your own community.

A church, an employer or a community organisation can raise a pool that funds scholarships for people connected to it. You know who in your community is ready for this. We do the funding, the checking and the reporting.

How a partner pool works

Money raised through your organisation is tracked as your pool and supports eligible scholars connected to your community. Every 2,500 dollars in that pool funds one twelve month scholarship.

So a congregation that raises 12,500 dollars has funded five scholarships. That is arithmetic rather than a projection: the number is simply the pool divided by the cost of a scholarship.

You get a straight account of what your pool did. How much came in, how many scholarships it funded, how those scholars are progressing, and what has been paid out. Where a scholar has agreed to share their progress, you will see it. Where they have not, you will see the totals and not the person.

What a partner does, and what we do

This split is deliberate, and it is the part most worth reading before you commit. Raising money for a community does not come with the power to pick who in it gets funded.

You can

  • Tell your community the free training exists.
  • Encourage people to register their interest.
  • Help someone get ready to apply.
  • Raise a scholarship pool for your community.
  • Follow the progress of scholars who agreed to share it.
  • Tell us about a support need you have noticed.

We do

  • Set who is eligible.
  • Apply the same standard to every applicant.
  • Check for conflicts of interest.
  • Make the award.
  • Hold and control the money.
  • Approve the provider, verify the training and approve every payment.

A partner cannot name a person and require that we fund them. Selection has to be even handed and independent of who raised the money, and a program that bent on that point would not be worth your community's trust.

What we are not ready to do yet

We are not taking payments through this site. There is no partner dashboard to log into yet, and no partner agreement to sign. This page starts a conversation with a person.

Career Pathways Network does not yet have federal tax exempt status, so contributions are not tax deductible at this time. If that matters to your organisation's giving, and it reasonably might, it is worth waiting until it is resolved.
