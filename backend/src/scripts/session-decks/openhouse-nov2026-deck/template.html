Think like an architect
A coder asks "how do I build it?"

An architect asks
"what is it allowed to do?"

  • What problem is actually worth solving?
  • What is it allowed to do, and what is it never allowed to do?
  • Who approves the risky actions?
  • What happens when it fails, and how do we know?
  • How do we prove it worked, in business numbers?

A support agent with a refund tool refunded $2,400 to an account created six minutes earlier, with a changed shipping address, after four other refunds that day. Every fact was in the system. Nothing was responsible for checking them. Every line of code ran correctly.
