What is deliberately absent: no readiness score, no proficiency percentage, no band numbers, no XP, no cohort dashboard styling, no email address. Plan §24 forbids raw gamification on an employer-facing surface, and the snapshot payload already strips bands and proficiency before they could reach here. An employer sees a capability, its provenance, and the work — nothing that looks like a grade.
The footer line is load-bearing. “A fixed snapshot. Later work does not change this page.” It tells an employer what they are looking at, and it is the visible half of the immutability guarantee the database already enforces.

Before I build

Four things I would rather you decided

  1. “Request an introduction” — build it or drop it? The plan wants contact details withheld behind an intro request. That needs a notification path and a place for Quincy to accept. Cheapest v1: the button emails you, and you broker it manually.
  2. Who reviews? I built it behind requireAdmin. If mentors should review, the queue needs the mgmt_role bridge instead.
  3. Featured builds — who picks? The mockup shows 6 of 54 selected. Nothing selects them yet. Either the learner curates, or v1 just shows the most recent N.
  4. Should the public page be indexable by Google? Plan §58 says only on explicit opt-in. Default I would ship: noindex until a learner asks for otherwise.
None of these block me starting. Surfaces 1 and 2 are fully specified by what already ships. I would build those first and bring the public page back to you once these are settled.
