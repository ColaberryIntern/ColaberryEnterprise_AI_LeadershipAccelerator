### Renewal reminders: stop mailing students the enrollment says are not there (2026-08-25)

- [x] **Gave the payment-reminder job a way to tell a live obligation from a stale subscription row.**
  - Date: 2026-08-25
  - Session: CC-20260825-q7wd
  - What changed: The job's entire notion of "this person owes us money" was `subscriptions.status = 'active'`. Nothing in this system retires a subscription row when a student withdraws, is deferred to a later cohort, or has notifications explicitly paused, and `loadActiveSubscriptions()` joined `enrollments` for `email` and `full_name` while contributing **zero** predicates. Added three gates to the pure selector (`renewalReminderSelection.ts`), fed by three columns now carried on the row: `enrollment_status` (anything other than `active` skips), `notifications_paused_at` (wired through the codebase's existing `isNotificationSuppressed()` helper, which already governed session reminders but had **never** been connected to money mail), and `access_starts_at` (the postponed-cohort-move gate; a future date means the programme has not started, so there is no term to renew). The gates sit **above** the date-window maths deliberately: a live send calls `startCheckout()`, which POSTs to PaySimple and inserts a `pending` subscription row, so excluding late would still mint a checkout in a deferred student's name. All three are additive, treating null/absent as notifiable, so the existing book is unchanged until somebody is deliberately withdrawn, paused or deferred.
  - Verification: **142 tests / 4 suites green** in `src/services/renewal/__tests__`, 10 of them new, covering all three gates in both directions plus the additive-default boundary for each. A regression test reproduces the real 2026-08-25 shape (paid, deferred to November, subscription row left active with its period ending inside the window) and asserts it is excluded **before** a checkout link could be minted. Type-checking evidence is CI's `Backend typecheck` job passing, alongside Backend unit tests, Frontend build, Frontend typecheck and all three security scans. A local `tsc --noEmit` in the worktree exits 2, but only on two pre-existing `TS2307: Cannot find module '@anthropic-ai/sdk'` errors in files this change does not touch, caused by the worktree's junctioned `node_modules` being a sparse install. Recorded rather than quietly dropped: a local typecheck that cannot resolve the dependency tree is not evidence, and CI with a real `npm ci` is. PR: https://github.com/ColaberryIntern/ColaberryEnterprise_AI_LeadershipAccelerator/pull/1825
  - Notes: **These gates do not by themselves explain the complaint that found them.** Kepha Ohanga's deferral was recorded on a **sibling enrollment row** (November cohort, `access_starts_at 2026-11-12`) marked `withdrawn`, while the row carrying his active subscription (July cohort) was `active` with no deferral marker at all, so the gates would not have caught him. He was the only member of the November cohort marked `withdrawn` while the other 13 were `active`. Exposure was measured rather than assumed: all 45 active subscriptions sat on `active` enrollments, and he was the only enrollment in the entire database with a future `access_starts_at`. So this shipped as hardening against a latent defect, not a live mass-billing bug. What it does provide is a clean, reversible, non-financial lever (`notifications_paused_at`) that previously had no effect whatsoever on payment mail.
