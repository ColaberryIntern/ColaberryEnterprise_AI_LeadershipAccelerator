## What was found (read-only)

1. **Nov Open House look = a hand-designed custom deck, not a day kind.** Session 1 (Oct 29,
   bc01abe2…) and Session 2 (Nov 12, ed37e664…) both render `kit_config_json.customDeck`, a
   self-contained HTML file (`session-decks/openhouse-nov2026-deck/template.html`; Session 2's
   source is not in the repo). A custom deck replaces the generated Class Kit wholesale: the
   audit, paste-prompt Build Bay, slideNotes and the two-screen presenter split do not apply to
   it. The look is reachable without the openhouse day kind (Session 2 has no "Open House" in its
   title), but only by leaving the build-class-deck contract. This build uses the generated
   Class Kit with Open House pacing; the custom-deck skin is an open decision.
2. **July Week 10 hands-on** = `session20-week10-monday.js` / `session21-week10-thursday.js`:
   paste prompts with a folder rule, ⏱ in the label, `# WHY` comments, expected/stop/rescue,
   from-empty starts, paste-then-ADVANCE while it runs. Applied to all three prompts here.
3. **QR** = `/portal/class-checkin/<session id>` (`sessionCheckinUrl`), on the cover and pinned
   to the corner of every slide (Q = full screen). The page is public; voting, pulse and
   questions need a portal sign-in (emailed link, no password). Enrolled students can take part
   in a normal session; guest join is Open-House-only.
4. **Side effects of a scheduled session in the cohort (83 active enrollments):**
   - creation: a Commons room row (RoomCreated is a no-op). No mail.
   - 24 h and 1 h before: reminder email to every active enrollment, unless
     `reminder_24h_sent_at` / `reminder_1h_sent_at` are set. Clean per-session suppression.
   - 15 min before start: status → live, check-in QR posted into the session's Commons room.
   - 30 min after the end: status → completed, every non-attendee marked absent and sent a
     missed-session email, absence alerts for 2+ misses, readiness recompute, AI recap.
     **No per-session flag**: avoided only if the status leaves `scheduled` before
     2026-11-24 18:15 CT.
