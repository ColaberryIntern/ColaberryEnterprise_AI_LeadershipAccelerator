## The seven phases are complete

The full journey now runs end to end: Today card → administrative intake → interview (guided form
or AI phone call, switchable mid-way) → applicant review and correction → submission → Dhee's
review queue → decision with a student-safe reason and an idempotent email → offer-letter package →
manual signature and upload → reviewer verification → payment gate → cohort membership without
touching the training cohort → active intern with a command card, an onboarding checklist and a
profile section. Existing interns convert through a dry run and a commit.

## Known limitations, carried forward deliberately

- Weekly-hour logging, curriculum-lag aggregation and certification-practice recency are **not
  instrumented**. They are declared `unknown` rather than shown as zero.
- Orientation attendance and community membership are read as `false` in the checklist; neither
  blocks activation.
- `internship_page_view` / `internship_application_started` are emitted to
  `student_navigation_events` but are **not yet wired into Explorer Growth scoring** — that needs a
  weight/half-life/cap decision. `STILL_UNINSTRUMENTED` should be updated in the same change.
- The whole funnel is behind `INTERNSHIP_ENABLED`, default **off**.
