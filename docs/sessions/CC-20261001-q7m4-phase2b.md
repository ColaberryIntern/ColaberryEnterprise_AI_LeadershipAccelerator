- [x] P2-T5 (part 1 of 2): stable source identity and provenance
  - Date: 2026-10-01
  - Session: CC-20261001-q7m4
  - What changed: `services/lifecycle/sourceIdentity.ts` — the six-state `SourceState` vocabulary, `mintSourceItem` / `reviseSourceItem` / `confirmSourceItem`, `isSameItem`, `compareSourceSets` and `itemsMissingProvenance`.
  - Verification: `jest -c jest.ci.config.ts src/services/lifecycle/__tests__/sourceIdentity.test.ts` — **24/24 passed, 11.7s**. `tsc --noEmit` matches the baseline: 4 pre-existing `playwright` errors, zero new.
  - Notes: **Identity is minted once and is NOT derived from the text.** Two consequences, both asserted: a typo correction preserves the id and bumps the revision (text-derived identity would make every wording fix look like a deletion plus an insertion, destroying the traceability the lifecycle exists for), and two items with *identical* text get *different* ids (two stakeholders saying the same sentence are two statements with possibly different provenance, and collapsing them loses one). `factoryIds.factoryId` stays the right tool for a *derived* id; it is the wrong tool for a captured human statement, which has no reproducible inputs, only a history.
  - Notes: **Confirmation cannot be manufactured.** An item may not be *captured* as `confirmed` — confirmation is an act performed on something already captured, so claiming it at capture time is the invented confirmation LC-02 forbids. `confirmSourceItem` requires a named actor, because "confirmed" with nobody attached is indistinguishable from "nobody contradicted it", and those are exactly the two meanings the request insists stay apart. A material re-wording **drops a confirmation back to `heard`**: carrying `confirmed` through an edit is how a changed requirement keeps a stale approval.
  - Notes: **Provenance is `null` when unrecorded, never guessed.** Legacy items keep their citation and carry null, and `itemsMissingProvenance` surfaces them so the gap is visible rather than filled with something plausible. `null` means unrecorded; it does not mean "assume the interview".
  - Notes: `compareSourceSets` is the LC-02/LC-03 round-trip check, and it reports **all** failure modes at once — loss, duplication and invented confirmation — rather than stopping at the first. **One test encodes the measured regression directly: 30 items in, 13 out, asserting 17 named as lost.** That is the exact shape of the failure that was fixed upstream (30 stated → 13 captured), so a future prompt or ceiling change cannot silently reintroduce it. It is a *set* comparison on ids and states, never a diff of generated prose: asserting exact wording would fail the first time a prompt is tuned and would teach everyone to ignore it.
  - Notes: Six positive controls across the suite, including one proving `compareSourceSets` can return `ok: false` at all — guarding against a future edit that makes it unconditionally green.
