## What was decided

| Area | Rule |
|---|---|
| Tuition | $149/mo billed annually ($1,788/yr) or $199/mo billed monthly. **Difference is the billing term, not the content.** |
| Third-party costs | Roughly $30/mo paid by the student directly: Anthropic subscription for Claude Code (~$20) and own-key LLM API usage (usually under $10). Not covered by tuition. |
| Duration | 12 weeks is the structured core of an open-ended monthly membership, not the end of it. |
| Internship | A membership inclusion, not a separate product. Unpaid, roughly two real Colaberry AI projects at a time, KPI-tracked, no fixed end date, runs until the person lands a full time AI role with one to two weeks notice. Cannot be employed full time elsewhere. |
| Internship fee waiver | Waived for anyone already paying Colaberry (current Data Analytics student or IPBC), or referred by Ram, or approved by Ali. Everyone else pays the membership. |
| Lead ownership | Sohail owns the Meta Ads funnel end to end. Dheeraj documents the process rather than running the follow up. |
| Admissions language | Cora's existing "internship support included in both plans" line was checked and is accurate. No correction needed. |
