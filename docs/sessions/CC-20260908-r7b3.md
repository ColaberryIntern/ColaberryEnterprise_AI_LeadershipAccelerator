## Class Dashboard: movement, put where it was asked for

Ali: *"ok let's make it so that everyone can see it - deploy"*, then, when I read that as an
access question: *"i mean without adding the tag to the back of the URL."*

**I had built the pace KPI in the wrong place.** His original spec said where it belonged —
*"One thing I can't really see in **my Class Dashboard** is movement"* — and it shipped inside
the Curriculum tab. That tab is a cohort drill-down, so the only routes to it were clicking a
cohort card and then Curriculum, or opening a hand-written `?tab=curriculum&cohort=<id>` link.
That link is what he was asking to be rid of.

`StudentPacePanel` now carries the bands, the student table and the week-by-week drill-down,
and renders on the Class Dashboard — which is what a cohort card's primary click opens.
`CurriculumCompletionTab` drops to the card tree alone, 269 → 190 lines. The split is by
question: *which parts of the curriculum are people finishing* stays in the Curriculum tab,
*where is each student* is the panel.

### `?view=pace`

The two consumers want opposite halves of one response, and the halves are wildly uneven.
Measured in the production container against the July 2026 cohort:

| Response | Bytes |
|---|---|
| Full | 288,513 |
| `?view=pace` | 8,385 |

The card tree is 1,293 cards and 97% of the payload, and the pace panel reads none of it. The
panel renders on the cohort landing view, so without the param every admin opening a cohort
would pay a quarter of a megabyte to read four numbers.

Both directions are tested, because both failures are silent: leaving the tree in breaks
nothing visible, and dropping `pace`/`students` from the pace response empties the KPI without
an error. Neither would be caught by looking at the screen.

### Two wrong claims I made getting here

Recorded because both were confidently stated and neither was true.

1. **`ai_staff` is not 23 instructors.** All 23 are `is_ai_operated = true` AI agent accounts —
   `corybrain@`, `inboxcaseengine@`, `governancestrategyarchitect@` and the rest of the
   architect fleet. Only 12 `admin_users` rows are human: 2 `super_admin`, 5 `admin`, 5 `sales`.
   I had put "add ai_staff" in front of Ali as "the instructors and mentors who actually run
   cohorts", and he chose it on that description. Implementing it would have handed 23 bot
   identities a screen that names every student and labels 41 of them behind.
2. **"The Accelerator home renders zero cohort cards" was my probe, not the product.** I had
   guessed the payload shape; the endpoint returns `{as_of, current, starting_soon, totals}`
   and July 2026 was in `current` the whole time.

### Cert Prep lists only people who have started

The bar is **`answered_total > 0`**, deliberately not `overall_state`.

Readiness is computed on a schedule, so a student who answered fifty questions this morning
still reads `not_measured` until the job runs. Filtering on state would hide exactly the
people who are working — the opposite of what was asked. A test pins that case as
load-bearing.

The July 2026 cohort has 57 rows and **every one is at zero attempts**, so the table was 57
lines of "Not measured". It now says that in one sentence and points at the never-started
panel. KPIs lead with Practising and Attempts.

### The Command Center is not local-only

The question was whether it could be linked at all. It can:

- it is a **GitHub Pages site at the root of the student's own repo**, so it is a public URL;
- the address is stored server-side by `setCommandCenterUrl`, **inside the
  `project_variables` JSON blob** rather than as a column — which is why searching the
  `projects` schema for `command_center_url` returns nothing and briefly looked like the
  feature did not exist;
- **11 of 43 live projects have one.** Verified in production, e.g. Farhat Beig at
  `fbeig2020-cloud.github.io/Kashmir-Craft-AI-Order-Assistant` and Jude Mofunanya at
  `jude-projects.github.io/AI-Project`.

The roster link renders only for projects that have a URL, rather than showing a dead button
on the other 32 rows.

### A dead link I had shipped

Card titles in the heatmap pointed at `/admin/curriculum?card=<id>`. **There is no
`/admin/curriculum` route in `adminRoutes.tsx`** — every card title was a 404, in the version
that went to production the day before. Inherited from the code the heatmap replaced, but
shipped again under my name.

They now point at the Composer. It reads only `?tab=` and `?type=`, so it cannot focus a
