- [x] Podcasts and testimonials on Today earn points at 75%
  - Date: 2026-09-11
  - Session: CC-20260910-r7k2
  - What changed: Podcasts and testimonials in the Today feed are AMBIENT items (`podcast:<id>`, `testimonial:<id>`) — provider picks with no `timeline_card` row — so the card watch gate, keyed on a card id, could never see them. Read-only probe on prod: **5,628 podcast and 2,988 testimonial placements across 201 students**, none of which could earn a point. New `ambientMediaGateService` mirrors the blog read gate's shape (beat → verdict → collect, own store `today_media_watch` via additive boot DDL) and **reuses the card gate's math** (`accumulateWatch` / `meetsWatchRequirement`) so 75% on a podcast means exactly what it means on a video. Routes `/api/portal/runtime/today/media/:kind/:id/{watch,collect}`. Collect is idempotent through `pointsService.award` keyed on the feed ref. The tile's podcast `<audio>` now emits beats through a new `useMediaBeats` hook carrying the same accumulator `VideoEmbed` uses; testimonial video already emitted beats and is now routed to the media gate; the drawer hydrates the ambient verdict on open and routes its beats the same way. `TodayShell` dispatches collect by ref prefix in one place (`collectCard`), replacing two duplicated handlers.
  - Verification: Backend `ambientMediaGateService.test.ts` 8/8 — accumulation to 75%, per-ref keying, seek-is-not-listening, 422 before the bar, 35 vs 10, idempotent double-collect, kill switch. `ambientMediaPoints.test.ts` 9/9. Frontend `useMediaBeats.test.tsx` 5/5 (cadence, seek ignored, paused does not accrue, tail flushed on pause, no empty beats); `TimelineCard.gamification.test.tsx` 6/6 (stamped reward shown, hydration keyed on provider id, unlocked copy, never calls the card gate for an ambient ref, completed items untracked, testimonial pays 10). Composer suites 28/28 unaffected.
  - Notes: **Points are stamped onto items by the server at serve time** (`stampAmbientMediaPoints`, pure, called on both serve paths). This is the append-only-snapshot trap from #2394/#2441 closed up front — the thousands of frozen `points: {}` impressions get their reward on next serve with no backfill — and it makes the tile's number and the gate's award one source of truth. The stamp lives in a pure module (`ambientMediaPoints.ts`) with no I/O imports precisely so the composer's enumerating test mocks do not break on load, which is what happened with `rehydrateProjectItems` this morning.
