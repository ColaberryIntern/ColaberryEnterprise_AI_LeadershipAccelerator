  - Date: 2026-09-22

  - What changed:
    - Ali, reading the live queue after the conversion: "I want them to have a status that is not open
      once they are approved. Open makes me think they are still going through the enrollment process."
      The `all_open` bucket meant "not rejected/withdrawn/removed/completed", so all eight ACTIVE interns
      sat in "All open 17" beside people who had not been interviewed.
    - `internshipReviewQueue.ts`: `all_open` → **`in_review`**, now ending at the human decision
      (not_started … waitlisted). Three buckets added: **`onboarding`** (documents_verified,
      payment_pending, activation_pending), **`active_interns`** (active, paused) and **`converted`**
      (the `converted_from_existing_intern` flag, non-closed). `OPEN_STATES_EXCLUDED` renamed
      `CLOSED_STATES`. The four state buckets PARTITION every non-closed state; `BUCKET_STATES`,
      `CLOSED_STATES` and `PARTITION_BUCKETS` are exported so a test can assert it.
    - `internshipTrackingService.ts`: the "Converted existing interns" KPI drilled into `all_open`, so
      its count said 8 and the list it opened showed all 17 open applications. Now drills into the new
      `converted` bucket. Pre-existing bug, found by this change.
    - `internshipRoutes.ts` bucket enum, `adminInternshipApi.ts` `QueueBucket`, and `InternshipQueue.tsx`
      chips (lifecycle reading order: In review → Awaiting review … → Docs pending → Onboarding →
      Active interns → Converted). The empty-state message keyed on `counts.all_open === 0`, which would
      have lied once `in_review` stopped meaning "everything"; it now checks every bucket.
