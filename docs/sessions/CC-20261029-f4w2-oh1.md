      - Every registration enrolls the person in the countdown (`enrollInOpenHouseCountdown`).

    - **`schedulerService.ts`:**
      - **AI context:** a countdown step is written from the event only. There is no cohort, no seats and no booking link, and the person's platform activity is included (`openHouseCountdownContext.ts`).
      - **Copy screen:** the draft must pass `screenEventReminderCopy`:
        - no price, offer, discount, "free" or seats;
        - the Join link present;
        - no other links.
        - Otherwise the fallback is sent and the reason is recorded in `metadata.ai_rejected`.
      - **Event start:** any countdown step still pending at the start is cancelled (`event_already_started`).
      - **Daily cap:** countdown steps are exempt from the cross-campaign daily cap. Its UTC day would push the T-1 hour email past the event.
      - **Signature:** Maya signs as Director of Admissions, and the Join link gets the button.
      - **Old sweep:** the plain reminder sweep stands down once the campaign is active. Until then it is the safety net, and it sends the Open House email, not the class template.
    - **`aiMessageService.ts`:** `eventContext` replaces the sales rules with Open House rules: the Join link is the only link, and there is no price, offer, booking or cohort.
    - **`liveSessionAttendanceService.ts`:** anyone registered for an Open House can check in to it, whatever their cohort (`maySelfJoin`).
    - **`scripts/ensureOpenHouseCountdownCampaign.ts`:**
      - Dry run by default. It never activates and never sends.
      - It marks colaberry.com verified only from live DNS.
      - It creates Maya's sender profile ("Maya at Colaberry" <maya@colaberry.com>), the 3-email sequence (T-2 days, T-1 day, T-1 hour) and the campaign as a draft, submitted for approval.
      - `--enroll-registrants` queues sendable registrants.
      - `--drafts N` writes real AI drafts for Ali to read.
      - `--attest` records Ali's two manual activation sign-offs, on his word only.
