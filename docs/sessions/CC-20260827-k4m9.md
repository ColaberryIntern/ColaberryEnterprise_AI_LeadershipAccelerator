### Instructor phone gets a preface before the read-aloud, and watches the room answer (2026-08-27)
- [x] **Ali test-drove the Session 11 deck ninety minutes before teaching it. Five changes: the read-aloud paragraph moved below the diagram, the instructor's phone now shows set-up direction on arrival and the read-aloud only on zoom, prompts announce themselves before he starts reading them, live poll results reach his phone, and the "student from last cohort" stories are gone because this IS the first cohort.**
  - Date: 2026-08-27
  - Session: CC-20260827-k4m9
  - What changed:
    - **`kitHtml.ts` — the lead paragraph moved below the diagram** on teach slides. It is the text the instructor reads out loud, so putting it at the top handed the room the next two minutes of narration before it was spoken. New order: title → key points → prompt → diagram → lead. It stays on the slide, because the slide is the record and the recording needs it.
    - **`kitHtml.ts` — new exported `promptBrief(slide)`**, derived entirely from fields the slide already authors: where the prompt runs, its Claude Code mode, what it is, what it produces, when you are done, and the rescue. Emitted per slide as `prompt_brief` in the deck's `__KIT__` model, computed independently of the existing `prompt` field because that one only populates on build-mode slides — the build map's primitive-map prompt sits on a `present`-mode slide and would otherwise never be announced.
    - **`kitDeckScript.ts` — the broadcast splits in two.** `presenter_preface` (prompt brief + the authored cue) and `presenter_tip` (the slide's own paragraph) now go over separately, where previously they were concatenated into one blob shown only on full-screen.
    - **`sessionKitDocService.ts` — the phone page has two modes.** Landed on a slide: the preface, in a deliberately lighter weight. Diagram full-screened: the read-aloud paragraph, large and bold. The old "Full-screen the diagram — your script appears here" dead state is gone; the page is now useful from the moment you arrive.
    - **Live poll results on the phone.** `getPresenterNotes` gained a `poll` block reusing `getPollTally`, and the page renders bars, counts and a percentage while a question is on screen.
    - **`week5.ts` — all three "last cohort" story beats recast** as ordinary workplace incidents (a logistics coordinator, two engineers on a team, a developer), matching the voice the deck's other beats already use.
  - Verification: **backend `tsc --noEmit` clean. `npx jest src/services/classKit` — 523/523 across 11 suites** (was 515/10; +8 covering the new behaviour). New `promptBrief.test.ts` pins that the brief is never silent, defaults its target to Claude Code, keeps its line order, omits lines the slide did not author, does not describe a terminal block as a Claude Code paste, and marks a read-along as nothing to paste. `kitDeckScript.test.ts`'s presenter block rewritten for the split. **The phone page's inline `<script>` was extracted and run through `node --check`** — TypeScript cannot see inside that template literal, and a syntax error there is invisible until it is on a phone in a live class. Deck re-rendered: **14/14 teach slides now place the lead paragraph after the diagram, 0 remaining "last cohort" references.**
  - Notes: **The poll block on the phone is read-only by construction** — counts and bars, no option buttons, no per-student identity — so the instructor watching the room form an answer can never turn into the instructor voting in it. That is a deliberate difference from `getLiveState`'s poll, which also returns `correctResponders`; the phone does not get names. **The layout change is repo-wide, not Week 5 only**: every teach slide in every week now renders its lead paragraph under the diagram. That is the intended scope — the reason it looked wrong tonight applies to every class — but it is the one change here that will be visible in decks nobody reviewed tonight.
