### The R2 diagnosis — four independent reasons `revenue_opportunities` is empty

R2 was revised to start with a diagnosis rather than a write. The result: **both existing
writers are unreachable AND both predicates are unsatisfiable. Fixing any one alone still
yields zero rows.**

**Writer 1 — `RevenueOpportunityAgent`**

1. `revenueOpportunityAgent.ts` registers itself as a module-load side effect
   (`registerAgent({...})`). **No module imports it** — nor any of the other ten files in
   `services/agents/reporting/`. There is no glob or dynamic loader, so `registerAgent` never
   executes. `cronRegistryBaseline.ts:92` already records it as `confirmed_dead`.
2. Its `ai_agents` row nevertheless claims `trigger_type=cron`, `schedule=0 */8 * * *`,
   `status=idle`, `run_count=0`, `error_count=0`, `last_run_at=null`. **Zero errors because
   zero runs** — every health surface misreads that as healthy.
3. It filters `pipeline_stage IN ('qualified','proposal','negotiation')`. The real vocabulary
   is `new_lead, contacted, enrolled, meeting_scheduled, negotiation, proposal_sent`. **Two of
   the three literals are not values of that column.**
4. Even corrected, the populations are near-disjoint: 1,100 leads score >= 70 (161 `new_lead`
   + 939 `contacted`), all in excluded stages; the 3 late-stage leads all have
   `lead_score = 0`. Measured match count: **0**.

**Writer 2 — `OpenclawConversionDetectionAgent`**

- It *is* wired (`aiOrchestrator.ts:63`, exported at line 616) but **nothing calls the exported
  wrapper** — zero callers.
- It has **no `ai_agents` row at all**, so its runs would be invisible to every health surface
  (the inverse of writer 1's problem).
- It needs conversations at `current_stage = 5`. All **138** `openclaw_conversations` rows sit
