- [x] C126: Today on a phone rendered 6x wider than the screen; the video opened off-screen behind a dimmed page
  - Date: 2026-11-14
  - Session: CC-20261029-f4w2
  - What changed:
    - **Reported:** on his phone Ali saw only the left part of Today, had to scroll sideways to find the video, and the page was dimmed. Most ad-funnel learners arrive on a phone.
    - **Measured on production** at iPhone width (390px), as a real Explorer learner through read-only View as member:
      - `/portal/today` was 2479px wide.
      - The phone browser zoomed its layout to 1560px.
      - The video drawer docks to the right edge of a full-screen dim layer (`.tld-scrim`, `position:fixed; inset:0`), so it landed off-screen.
      - The other 12 portal pages and 4 public pages fit.
    - **Cause:** below 1300px `.te-grid` collapsed to `grid-template-columns:1fr`.
      - A bare `1fr` is `minmax(auto,1fr)`, so the track would not shrink below its widest child: it was 2465px inside a 362px box.
      - The Sep 4 fix (ab3191fdf) covered the page shell's grid; this inner grid came with the Oct 26 Explorer home base (C54).
    - **Fix (`frontend/src/pages/portal/today/TodayShell.css`):**
      - `.te-grid` collapses to `minmax(0,1fr)`.
      - `.te-grid>*{min-width:0}`.
      - The two shell one-column rules are also `minmax(0,1fr)`, so the file has no bare `1fr` left.
    - **New `scripts/auditPhoneView.js`:**
      - Loads public pages signed out, and portal pages as a member through read-only View as member, at 390px with touch, in Edge.
      - Per page it reports: sideways scroll, the outermost elements sticking out, any full-screen dim layer, and a screenshot.
      - Exits 1 if any page scrolls sideways.
      - Tokens are scrubbed from errors.
  - Verification:
    - **Live, before deploy:** the fix was injected into the production page in the phone browser. The page went from 2479px to 390px, and the video drawer was on screen and playing (screenshots taken).
    - **`todayPhoneWidth.test.ts`:** 3 of 3 pass. Restoring the old rule fails 2 of 3.
    - **Typecheck:** the new test file passes `tsc --strict`, with a planted control error reported. The full local frontend `tsc` cannot run in this worktree (TS 4.9 stops on newer d3 types through the junctioned node_modules), so CI's frontend typecheck is the gate.
