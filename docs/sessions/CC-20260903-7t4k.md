  - Date: 2026-09-03

  - Verification: `tsc --noEmit` exit 0. 20/20 new tests green; 100 Cert Prep tests total under `jest -c jest.ci.config.ts`.
  - Notes: **The 0–1000 number is honest about what it is not.** Anthropic produces CCAR-F's scaled score through criterion-referenced equating we do not have and cannot reproduce. Ours is a published LINEAR transform so a practice result sits on the same axis and can be compared to the same 720 line, and the file header states it must never be captioned as a predicted Anthropic score. The consequence is documented rather than buried: under a linear map **720 lands at 68.9% correct** — a reasonable working bar and an assumption, not a fact about the real exam. Being linear and published means a student who asks "why 640?" gets an answer; an opaque curve could not give one. **An unanswered question is not a wrong answer** — skipped items are excluded from both numerator and denominator in the per-domain roll-up, because counting them wrong makes an abandoned session look like a failed one and someone who ran out of time has not demonstrated they do not know the material. A test asserts the slots sum exactly across eight form sizes; the official weights produce 16/11/12/12/9 = 60. Unweighted domains are DROPPED rather than given an equal share — inventing a share is exactly the false precision the nullable schema exists to prevent, so the caller checks `weightsAreUsable` and offers unweighted practice instead of a weighted mock.
