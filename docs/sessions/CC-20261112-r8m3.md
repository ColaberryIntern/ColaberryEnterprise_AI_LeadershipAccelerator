## The census that shaped the design (read-only against production, 2026-11-12)

Nothing below is inferred from a table name. Every line was a `SELECT`.

### Membership and Annualized were already one number

`revenueRoomService` fills the Membership card by calling `annualizedRevenue()`.
`commercializationBoardService`'s own `annualized_revenue` tile calls the same function.
R13 (PR #3928) merged that board into the Revenue Room, so since then **one computation has
been printing twice on one page under two names.** Ali's question had a factual answer:
combine them. Done — one stream, `Membership (annualized)`.

### Membership is the only stream with real dollars

| status | plan | rows | with paysimple |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| pending | monthly | 96 | 0 |
| pending | annual | 36 | 0 |
| failed | monthly | 25 | 0 |
| failed | annual | 17 | 0 |
| active | monthly | 26 | 26 |
| active | comp | 27 | 0 |
| active | annual | 6 | 6 |
| canceled | monthly | 35 | 34 |
| canceled | comp | 5 | 0 |
| canceled | annual | 1 | 1 |

**32 of 274 rows are billable.** 174 rows carry a plan and a price and no payment id. About
$72.8K annualized is real.

### There is no payments ledger

`information_schema` returned no `payments`, `invoices` or `transactions` table. The only
money-bearing tables are `subscriptions`, `refunds`, `account_credits`,
`marketing_spend_facts` and `subscription_renewal_reminders`. Consequences, both now stated
on the page rather than left to assumption:

- **Renewal history is not reconstructable.** `current_period_end` holds one date, the next
  boundary. A member who paid nine times looks identical to one who paid once. So
  `member_renewed` is forward-looking only and never appears in the historical timeline.
- **A failed payment has no date.** Nothing records when it failed; dating it from
  `updated_at` would put a guess on a timeline wearing a timestamp. Funnel state, not event.

### Government bids carry no value and no deadline

- `gov_qualifications` has 22 columns; not one is a value, budget, ceiling or close date.
- `source_snapshot` was the obvious candidate and is **NULL on all 14 flagged rows**.
- `gov_company_references.contract_value` describes OUR past contracts and holds 0 rows.
- `delivery_opportunities.value_score` is an integer capability score, not dollars.

The 14 rows at `approved_bid_pursuit AND active` include one named
`Snow College Adult Learner - T8 test`, `RFP - Enterprise Work Order Management System`
twice under two project ids, and three rows with no delivery project. `gov_submission` holds
**0 rows**: nothing has ever been submitted.

### The win/loss rails already existed and had never been used

`gov_submission` already carries `status`, `outcome`, `outcome_note`, `outcome_recorded_at`,
`externally_submitted_at`, `external_ref`, `acknowledged_at`, with
`SubmissionOutcome = pending | won | lost | withdrawn | no_bid | unknown`, a tested
`POST /outcome` route, and an EXPORTED-is-not-SUBMITTED honesty rail. Zero rows.

**So the funnel Ali described — flagged → priced → ready → exported → submitted →
acknowledged → won/lost — was entirely in the schema except "priced".** That is the whole of
what Phase 1 adds.

## What landed

- [x] `gov_bid_values` — the one missing table
  - Date: 2026-11-12
  - Session: CC-20261112-r8m3
  - What changed: new `backend/src/db/ensureGovBidValueSchema.ts`, wired into `server.ts`
    boot after `ensureGovSubmissionSchema` (it FKs `delivery_projects`, same as that one).
    Holds a human-confirmed proposal value per bid with its basis, basis strength, term in
    months, and who confirmed it. Corrections supersede rather than overwrite, so "the value
    was revised" is itself a dated event.
  - Verification: `tsc --noEmit` clean on all new files (see the tsc note below); 20 tests in
    `ensureGovBidValueSchema.test.ts` asserting the generated DDL carries every rail.
  - Notes: **The name was checked against `information_schema` on production before it was
    taken** — `gov_bid_values`, `bid_values`, `gov_bid_value`, `proposal_values` and
    `gov_proposal_values` all absent. That check is R14's lesson: `IF NOT EXISTS` protects a
    second RUN, never a second OWNER, and it cannot tell the two apart.

- [x] A price is a named human's claim, which unlocks the government card
  - Date: 2026-11-12
  - Session: CC-20261112-r8m3
  - What changed: `govBidValueService.ts`. `confirmedBy` is required, taken from the session
    and never from the request body. `value_basis` must be >= 25 characters and is enforced in
    the database too, so a direct psql insert cannot bypass it.
  - Verification: 22 tests, including every rejection path; 3 mutations planted and caught.
  - Notes: `revenueInitiatives.ts` has said since R1 that "there is no column anywhere that
    records 'a human confirmed this bid is live'", and named that as the government card's
    `unlockedBy`. **Pricing a bid IS that confirmation** — nobody attaches a dollar figure
    and a basis to a row they believe is a test. So "possible proposal winnings" sums only
    priced bids, and the test row, the duplicate and the three project-less rows cannot
    inflate it. The unpriced count travels beside the total so the gap stays visible.

- [x] One classification registry, so "every classification has a color" has one source
  - Date: 2026-11-12
  - Session: CC-20261112-r8m3
  - What changed: `revenueClassifications.ts` — 21 classifications across three streams, each
    with a tone, a glyph, a label, a direction and a `means` sentence.
  - Verification: 15 tests; mutation planted on `bid_flagged.carriesValue` and caught.
  - Notes: declares a **tone**, not a hex. The frontend maps five tones onto the design
    system's reserved status tokens, which is what `commercializationTiles` and
    `statusVocabulary` already do. `direction` is kept separate from `tone` because they
    answer different questions: a refund is danger AND out, a cancellation is danger AND
    none, and treating those the same double-counts the loss.

- [x] The two streams and the board
  - Date: 2026-11-12
  - Session: CC-20261112-r8m3
  - What changed: `membershipFlow.ts`, `govBidFlow.ts`, `revenueFlowTypes.ts`,
    `revenueFlowService.ts`, `revenueFlowController.ts`, three routes under the already
    section-gated `/api/admin/commercialization` prefix.
  - Verification: 66 tests across 4 suites; 265 regression tests green across
    `commercialization`, `revenueRoom` and the routes.
  - Notes: **the headline has no field holding booked revenue plus possible winnings**, and
    that absence is asserted in the tests. One figure is $72.8K that exists; the other is
    contract value that might arrive over terms that may run five years. Summing them would
    be the most misleading thing this page could do.

- [x] `annualizedRevenueCents()` extracted, so there is still exactly one revenue rule
  - Date: 2026-11-12
  - Session: CC-20261112-r8m3
  - What changed: `commercializationBoardService.annualizedRevenue()` now delegates to a new
    exported `annualizedRevenueCents()`.
  - Verification: 265 regression tests green; the only test that mocks the module passes
    unchanged.
  - Notes: the dashboard needs cents for arithmetic and cannot work from a formatted
    "$72,816". Re-deriving the SQL in the new caller was the alternative, and that function's
    own header warns against exactly it.

## Verification evidence

**`tsc --noEmit`, backend, TypeScript 5.7.3: clean on every file in this diff.**

Worth recording how that was established, because the first two attempts were worthless:

1. `npx tsc` in the worktree ran a **decoy package** — it printed "This is not the tsc command
   you are looking for" and **exited 0**. Trusting it would have meant reporting a clean
   typecheck on code it never read.
2. The root `node_modules/typescript` is **4.9.5**, too old to parse zod v4 and
   `@types/d3-dispatch`, so it emitted a flood of library syntax errors (TS1xxx, which
   `skipLibCheck` does not suppress) and never reached my files.
3. `backend/node_modules/typescript` is a broken partial install with no `package.json`, next
   to `.typescript-*` temp directories from an interrupted npm install.

Resolved by installing TypeScript 5.7.3 (the version `backend/package.json` declares) into the
scratchpad and running it against `backend/tsconfig.json`. **A deliberate control error was
planted first and confirmed caught** — `revenueFlowTypes.ts(164,14): error TS2322` — before
any clean result was believed.

Four errors remain, all pre-existing and environmental: `maxmind` (×2) and
`@anthropic-ai/sdk` (×2) are absent from the junctioned `node_modules`, in three files that
are not in this diff. CI installs them.

## What is deliberately NOT in this phase

- **The page.** Phase 2: dashboard hero, per-stream sparkline in the internship-report
  grammar (day columns, stacked colour bands, today outlined), one combined timeline,
  drill-through.
- **The pricing control.** Phase 3: the admin UI to enter a proposal value and record
  won/lost. Until a bid is priced the headline is an honest `14 flagged, none priced` rather
  than a number.
- **The five revenue hypotheses.** They have cards on the Revenue Room already, they are bets
  rather than revenue, and putting them on a board whose headline is money would invite them
  to be read as pipeline. Ali named two streams; this builds two.
