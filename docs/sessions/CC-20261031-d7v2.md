## Why this change exists

Chuks Eneh emailed on 30 Oct asking for help with his demo. His recording had in fact
arrived successfully, so the question looked like confusion about navigation. It was not.

Zoom chooses the composition of the file it saves from what actually happened in the
room: share a screen and it writes `shared_screen_with_speaker_view`, share nothing and
it writes `active_speaker`. Querying `presentation_recordings` on production:

| Presenter | Type | Screen shared |
|---|---|---|
| Ali Muwwakkil | `project_introduction` x3 | yes |
| Emmanuel Sane | `working_system_demo` x3 | **no** |
| Chukwuemeka Eneh | `working_system_demo` | **no** |

Four of four *student* demos came back with the learner talking and the system they
built nowhere in frame. The only recordings carrying a screen are staff ones. Four for
four is a product gap, not four careless people: nothing in the flow said so at the
moment it mattered.

## Why `GetReadyPanel` and nowhere else

`RecordingEvidencePicker` already flags a take that arrived without a screen, but by
then the take is spent, the room is closed and the 30-minute booking is gone.
`GetReadyPanel` is the last surface where the sentence can still change the outcome.

The line states the consequence rather than only the instruction. "Share your screen"
alone reads as optional; knowing the recording comes back unusable is what makes someone
do it before they start rather than three minutes in.

Unlike the two existing facts, it is **not** derived from the attempt's mode or the
room's recording policy, because it is true of every room and every presentation type.
A test pins that it survives `cohort_live`, `practice_peer`, and `recordingPolicy: never`.

## Verified separately, NOT addressed here

Every attempt in production sits at `attempt_state = 'scheduled'` — all 11, including
the 8 carrying successfully ingested recordings. A student who records still sees the
task as not started. Confirmed platform-wide by grouping all attempts rather than
reading one student's row. Called out on the Basecamp ticket; it needs its own fix and
is deliberately out of scope here.
