## Assumptions (logged, reversible)
1. **Match confidence %.** The suggestor's score is a raw integer (category 3 + 1 per keyword), not a percent. It is mapped deterministically onto the backend's own strength bands: strong (score >= 5) maps to 75+, moderate (3-4) to 50-74, weak to < 50 (`suggestionConfidence`).
2. **Quadrant split.** The quadrant splits at score 50 and at the median known open-match count, min 1. The mockup hardcoded 13.
3. **Gov-match color.** It is relative to the busiest service: top third danger, middle third warning.
4. **Fit links.** "Re-assess fit" links to `/admin/gov-qualification?gws=<qualificationKey>&tab=fit`. This assumes `qualification_key` is the workspace key (it is for both canonical and `gws:` keys today).
5. **Retire on growth.** Retire is offered on growth services too (it was active-only), per SV1's handoff ("retire a mistaken growth service").
6. **Outside Link label.** The Link button on an outside case study reads "Link (internal only)", per Ali's decision that outside case studies are internal only.
