# CC-20261015-09c8 — One student's error report, and the two production faults underneath it

Two student reports, four findings, and each one was found by refusing to stop at the
first plausible cause. The order matters, because none of it was the thing reported:

1. **"Add a story" failed for every student** — a partial unique index added three days
   earlier collided with the order of two statements inside `publishPlan`.
2. **The fix sat merged but undeployed for fourteen hours**, and reached production as a
   passenger on an unrelated team's deploy rather than by intent.
3. **A global kill switch, left on after that morning's deploy, was eating all outbound
   email** — including the alerts that would have reported it.
4. **`auditMiddleware` was writing plaintext staff passwords into `audit_logs`**, 639 rows
   of them, and was still doing so when it was found.

Findings 3 and 4 both came out of one WhatsApp message that said only "I am not getting
any enterprise access link in my mail".

## What the first one asked for, and what landed

Swati reported "Internal server error" from the project interior's **Add a story** form and
had done the triage herself: the project id, the repo, the plan version, the timestamps, and
two suspicions of her own (her wording, and the hand-edited `plan.json`). Both suspicions
were wrong, and the real cause was neither hers nor new to her — the same failure was waiting
for **every** student.

What landed:

- `backend/src/services/sbp/planStore.ts` — inside `publishPlan`'s existing locked
  transaction, the incumbent is **superseded first** and the target version promoted second.
  The order is load-bearing and it is the opposite of what it was.
- `backend/src/services/sbp/addStoryService.ts` — `MAX_STORIES_PER_BUILD` 30 to 50, at the
  DRI's instruction.
- Four tests across `__tests__/planStore.test.ts` and `__tests__/addStoryService.test.ts`
  that pin both, with the publish statements asserted by **content as well as position**.

Swati's own two stories were recovered by hand from her stranded drafts and are live
independently of this code change.

**Now deployed.** It reached production at **6:43 AM CDT on 2026-10-15** as a passenger on
another workstream's deploy of `fb236969e` (PR #3104), not on one of its own. Verified in
the running container rather than inferred from the merge: the compiled `publishPlan`
supersedes at line 204 and promotes at 207, and `MAX_STORIES_PER_BUILD` reads 50.
