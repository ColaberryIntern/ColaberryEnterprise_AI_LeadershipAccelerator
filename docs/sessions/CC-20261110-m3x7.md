### Design notes

- **The list is cheap, the message is not.** A full roster render of the report pipeline is a
  few hundred queries and about thirteen seconds. The tab therefore lists from the console
  roster it is handed and generates one intern's brief on expand, which also makes the
  freshness claim exact: the words describe them at the moment the row was opened.
- **Rows come from the shell, not from a query of its own.** The old view loaded from
  `agent_domain_projects`, which is why it showed seven of fourteen. Taking the shell's `rows`
  makes every intern appear and makes the All / Needs attention / No project / Paused chips
  work here for free, because the filtering already happened.
- **The deadline is read from `due_baseline_on`.** See `nudgeBrief/deadline.ts` for the
  measurement that forced it: `due_on` told us a task was fifty days late on a project that
  was fourteen days old.
- **What was approved is what is delivered.** The body is posted back from the textarea rather
  than regenerated at send time, and the sender's guards are re-checked on the edited text.
