  - Notes: The suggestion pass reuses `factory/serviceMatcher` rather than growing a second matching scheme, but deliberately does NOT reuse its strength banding, and that is the reason the suggestor module exists at all. That banding is calibrated to NAICS code overlap, weighted 10, with `'strong'` meaning "scored at least 10". `service_offerings` carries `naics_codes_json`; `case_studies` carries no NAICS column anywhere in the schema, confirmed with two positive controls after an `&&` short-circuit had hidden the control on the first attempt. So for a case study that term is always zero, `'strong'` is unreachable, and every good match would be labelled `'weak'` no matter how well it matched. The suggestor re-bands on the signals that do exist and exports its formula so a test can prove the NAICS term is inert, asserted on the CALL rather than the output because an output-level comparison could not fail for the reason it appeared to test.
