## What started it

Ali could not log in to ParentSquare and no reset email reached his Hotmail. The email
had in fact arrived twice, 07:41 and 08:48 Central, and the Inbox COS had archived both.

Production audit log, `inbox_classifications`:

```
09-28 08:48 | donotreply+277399fa…@parentsquare.com | Wylie ISD Connect: Reset Password
            | AUTOMATION | hard_rule | Sent from noreply address (forwarded from Hotmail)
09-28 07:41 | (same)                                | (same)
```

Section 5 of `evaluateHardRules` archives anything whose From matches
`/no[-_.]?reply|do[-_.]?not[-_.]?reply/`. ParentSquare sends from
`donotreply+<uuid>@parentsquare.com`, so every message it has ever sent was archived
unless an earlier rule happened to catch it first.

That "happened to" is the second half of the bug. Section 3's priority-keyword list
(`pta`, `field trip`, `parent teacher`, …) scans the body, so whether a school message
reached the inbox depended on which words landed in that day's digest:

| Date | Message | Result | Deciding factor |
|---|---|---|---|
| 09-25 | Digest: 2 new messages | INBOX | body contained "pta" |
| 09-24 | Digest: 2 new messages | INBOX | body contained "field trip" |
| 09-16 | Digest: 2 new messages | AUTOMATION | neither word present |
| 09-15 | Digest: 1 new message | AUTOMATION | neither word present |
| 09-22 | Absence Notification \| Addison | AUTOMATION | — |

Same sender, same kind of message, opposite outcomes.

Querying the class rather than the symptom showed the blast radius over 90 days:

- **66** school messages archived (ParentSquare + `do.not.reply@WylieISD.net`)
- **103** account-security messages archived — password resets, verification codes,
  OTPs and magic links. Among them eight **Hetzner** verification codes (the production
  VPS provider), Ceipal password-reset OTPs, a HeyGen magic link, DoorDash and Xcaret
  codes, Pylon portal codes.

Every one of those is time-limited and user-initiated: archived is the same as lost.

This is the fourth instance of one recurring defect — a wanted message reaching
sections 4/5 and being archived on a header or a sender shape. The file already carries
three prior fixes of the same shape (Cory briefings 2026-06-02, Basecamp @mentions
2026-06-24/29, Slack 2026-09-16). The two new rules are deliberately built like the
Slack one.
