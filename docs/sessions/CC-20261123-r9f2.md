## What discovery found, before any code

Worth recording because it changed the shape of the work twice.

**The four things Ali named are three.** Both "Maya campaigns" are the open house machinery:
`Open House Countdown (Maya)` and `Open House Follow-up (Maya)`, found by
`settings->>'campaign_key'` (`openHouseCountdown.ts:376`, `openHouseFollowup.ts:56`) rather than
by name. So the open house is not one of four inputs feeding revenue; it is the input, and the
two Maya campaigns are the engine that works its list. The alumni campaign is a separate motion:
`Colaberry Alumni Re-Engagement Campaign`, 922 sends into a 10,343-lead list, 1.53 percent open
rate, zero replies (`seeds/campaignGovernanceDeclarations.ts:113-136`).

**Most of the attribution already exists and is already on the page.** R19's `channelTruth`
computes exactly the path asked for: measured on production 2026-11-17, the open house produced
16 of the 26 paying members, CCPP alumni 5, winback 5, and paid ads produced none against
$3,894.80 of recorded spend. `ChannelTruthPanel` renders it at `AdminRevenueRoomPage.tsx:428`,
roughly sixty lines BELOW the flow board at line 370. The data is on the screen and divorced from
the funnel that produces the number.

**The page reads number-then-stages, which is the inverse of a path.** Each stream card prints
its headline figure above its funnel table (`RevenueFlowDashboard.tsx:283` before this phase).
Membership's first funnel stage is `signed_up`, meaning a `subscriptions` row already exists;
everything before that is missing from the funnel.

**The grain has to be channel, not campaign, and the numbers say why.** Coverage over the 26
currently-paying members, measured 2026-11-17 and recorded in `channelTruth.ts:9-26`:

```
leads.source ........ 26 of 26   complete
any UTM tag .........  7 of 26
utm_campaign ........  6 of 26
```

A path keyed on campaign is blank for 19 of the 26 people who pay us. And nothing anywhere
records that a subscription exists BECAUSE a campaign asked: `subscriptions` carries no campaign,
source or UTM column, only `enrollment_id`. `ideaFlow.ts:303-308` already says this in production
copy on the conversion stream's `wired` stage, which sits at 0.

Ali chose the two-band shape: band 1 names each campaign with its real reach and is explicitly
not revenue, band 2 shows channels with paying members, and a visible break between them says
where the chain of evidence stops.

### Three gaps found in the timeline on the way past

1. There is no per-source timeline anywhere. One global list with a stream filter.
2. `FlowTimeline`'s `onPickEntity` drill hook is declared, used internally at `:135`, `:158` and
   `:159-167`, and passed by no caller. Dead code.
3. `FlowTimeline` drops days past `maxDays = 40` with `.slice(0, maxDays)` and renders no notice.
   No caller overrides it, so a 180-day window silently shows the 40 most recent active days.
   That is a real honesty gap on a surface whose stated doctrine is that an empty tail must
   explain itself, and it is why `windowDays` travels in the payload at all. Phase 3 fixes it.
