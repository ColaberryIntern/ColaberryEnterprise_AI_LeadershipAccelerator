## Why the two tabs are their own section keys, not folded into `internship`

The hub lives behind the `internship` section because the application queue carries an applicant's
resume, phone number and interview transcript. Folding Agents and Gov Contracts into that same key
would hand both surfaces to Admissions (Dhee), who holds `internship` to run the queue and has no
business in the agent build surfaces or the government delivery projects. Minting the two keys keeps
them management-only (owner/admin) until the per-domain and per-project grants land in M1-M3, exactly
the reasoning AF2.3 used when it split `brands` out of `campaigns`.
