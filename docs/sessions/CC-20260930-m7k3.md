# Session CC-20260930-m7k3

**Date:** 2026-09-30
**Branch:** `fix/portal-header-real-logo` (cut from `origin/main`)
**Scope:** The portal header logo — Ram's "rough at the edges" flag, traced to a cause.

---

## What started it

Ram, over WhatsApp:

> You need to fix that logo at the top of the portal man. It immediately throws
> people off and makes them feel that we are not sophisticated, but rough at the
> edges type of people.

That reads like a matter of taste. It isn't. The header was drawing the brand
twice and drawing it wrong, and the repo's own brand guide already said so.

## What was actually on screen

`PortalShell.tsx` rendered this:

```jsx
<div className="te-brand">
  <img className="te-mark" src="/colaberry-icon.png" alt="Colaberry" />
  <div><b><span className="cc">C</span>olaberry</b><span>AI Systems Architect Accelerator</span></div>
</div>
```

Two separate problems, stacked:

1. **`/colaberry-icon.png` is the mono-black mark.** Stripped of colour and shown
   at 38px, the two cherries and their shared stem read as a *bicycle* — two
   wheels and a crossbar. It is not the cherry mark anyone would recognise.
2. **The wordmark was hand-set.** "Colaberry" typed in Quicksand, weight 700,
   with `.cc { color: var(--cherry) }` painting the `C` red.

Put together, the `C` appeared **twice** — once as the cherry mark, once as a
typed letter — which is the specific thing that makes a header look improvised.

`frontend/public/knowledge/design/BRAND.md` forbids it outright:

> The mark is **two cherries on a shared stem forming the "C"** of Colaberry.
> The wordmark sets **"olaberry"** in Quicksand with a green→blue gradient.
> […] Never recolor the cherries, add gradients/shadows to the mark, or
> **re-set the wordmark in another font**.
>
> **Minimum size:** 120px wide (horizontal lockup), 24px (mark alone).

## The fix was already sitting in the file

`TodayShell.css` carries a `.te-brand--site` modifier whose own comment states
the intent:

> 3B: the portal wears the SITE lockup so the crossing from /platform to /try is
> invisible, and keeps the product subtitle so a signed-in user still knows which
> room they are in.

It was written **for the portal**. Only `ManagementPreviewPage` ever consumed it.
A producer shipped without its intended consumer; the portal kept the placeholder.

## The dark-mode bug nobody had filed

Rendering the before/after in a real browser surfaced a second defect: on the dark
header (`--surface: #1E1E1E`) the mono-**black** mark is almost entirely
invisible. Light mode looked amateurish; dark mode was simply missing a logo.

## Asset decision

The obvious dark-mode move — swap to `colaberry-horizontal-white.png` — is wrong
here. That file, and the `-black` variant beside it, are a **stale logotype**:
capital "C", a different mark, a different wordmark. They are not mono cuts of the
current gradient lockup, so theming between them would ship *two different logos
depending on the theme*.

The current colour lockup has clean alpha — sampling its semi-transparent edge
pixels gives a mean RGB of `109,150,135`, i.e. artwork colour, not a white matte —
so it composites correctly on both surfaces. `colaberry-logo-transparent.png`,
by contrast, **is** matted against white and halos visibly on dark, which is why
`ManagementPreviewPage` moved off it too.

One asset now serves both themes.
