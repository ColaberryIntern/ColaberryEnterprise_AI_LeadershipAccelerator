## Failure modes

- **A step throws or is paused:** it is logged and the next step still runs. The Governor refuses unscored profiles, so a failed recompute produces WAIT rows with a reason, not decisions on old states.
- **One learner fails:** the batch collects it and continues.
- **The process restarts mid-sequence (a deploy):** the remaining steps do not run that night. There is no automatic retry. Each step is safe to rerun through its operator script, and the next night is complete.
- **Not handled:** a sequence lasting over 24 hours would overlap the next one.
