date, and the related artifact. Status values: `proposed` (awaiting the design gate), `locked`
(settled for Phase 1), `deferred` (revisit at a later gate). Approver for design-level decisions is
the DRI, Ali Muwwakkil, at Gate A. Conforms to `00-canonical-decisions.md`.
