4 · Art direction & accessibility

Art direction

  • Motif: a time-machine aperture built from concentric architectural blueprint rings receding into depth, with a single coral point of light at the center (the moment of decision).
  • Architecture: a faint isometric system map (nodes + links) and drafting-grid lines drift across the right two-thirds, signaling "system", not "robot".
  • Time: a horizontal dial reads BEFORE · AFTER · FUTURE, the spine of the whole experience.
  • Palette: Design-E only. Berry #367895 deep field, one coral accent #FB2832, amber horizon #E8920C. No neon, no glitch, no sci-fi chrome.
  • Type: Roboto wordmark, Roboto Mono for the dial, so it reads as a professional instrument.
  • Forbidden: robots, faces, game-UI, lens flares, stock "AI brain".

Accessibility review

  • Text is not load-bearing: the card title/label/meta live in the tile chrome (real DOM text), so the poster carries no information a screen-reader needs. The alt is decorative-descriptive: "Architect Time Machine — a blueprint portal receding into depth."
  • Contrast: wordmark is #FFFFFF on the darkest region (ratio > 12:1). The coral accent is never the sole carrier of meaning.
  • Theme-safe: baked dark background keeps ≥ AA contrast on both light and dark feeds.
  • Motion: the asset is a static JPG. The subtle drift shown here is prototype-only and is disabled under prefers-reduced-motion.
  • Small-size legibility: verified readable at 190px (mobile) — the aperture + wordmark survive; fine grid lines are allowed to fade.
