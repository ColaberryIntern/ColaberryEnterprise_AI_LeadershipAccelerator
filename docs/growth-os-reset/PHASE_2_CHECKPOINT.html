Question 1, answered with a number instead of "unknown" — and then largely repaired.

86 Free AI accounts were invisible to Explorer Growth. The backfill demanded positive free-signup provenance and excluded org members, prospect accounts, staff, agent identities and paid rows, which is why it planned only 56 writes (37 programme, 19 tier) out of 376 incoherent rows. Ali authorised it and it ran on 2026-10-13. Measured from the table: coherent explorer+guest went 0 → 56, invisible standard+guest went 86 → 49, and the 630 row total did not move. The two genuinely paid Explorers were untouched. A 56-entry rollback ledger sits on the host.

Two things it did not fix. All 56 coherent rows carry explorer_entered_at NULL and no task owns that column — the script's own warning names only the 37 it wrote into the programme, which understates it, since the column has never been populated for anybody (measured: 56 null, 0 set). And the remaining 49 include 37 the script reports as ambiguous rather than guessing at. Its success line also printed committed NaN of 56 — a real reporting defect, now fixed, and the reason the numbers above come from an independent query rather than from the script.

Nothing sends, now confirmed in production as well as in tests. GROWTH_JOURNEY_EXECUTION_ENABLED is unset on the running container. In tests, a verifier built a syscall-level tripwire patching net.connect, tls.connect and dns.lookup, with controls proving it blocks, and recorded zero vendor-host contact attempts across all 31,042 tests — still zero with the withholding logic mutated out, so the stub rather than the business logic is what enforces it.
