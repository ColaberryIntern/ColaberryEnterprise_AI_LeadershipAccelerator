The two deductions

Both are mine, and both are stated rather than smoothed.

The gate cannot catch a fabricated count  −3

The citation gate proves a quoted literal is real, findable and unique. It says nothing about a number written in prose — and I published three invented counts during this phase, each corrected in the record afterwards. The working rule now: a number reaches prose by being pasted from a command’s output, never retyped from memory of it.

Most artifacts are not independently graded  −7

Two tasks were graded by a verifier that did not write them. The remaining nine are committed but ungraded, because I batched verification to keep the phase to one sitting. Batching is a trade, not a free action. Recoverable without rework.

The headline findings

Three things change how the next phases should be planned.

Free accounts do not reliably enter Explorer

Two anonymous-reachable public paths write opposite labels, and the Explorer OS gates on only one. A whole population is invisible to it — no profile, no scores, no state, no decisions. For anyone who used both paths, the row chosen at login is the one Explorer cannot see.

Person 360 hides the score the system exists to produce

A person’s stage is derived from the mere existence of an enrollment row by email — no check of type, tier or payment — and that stage nulls their temperature and lead score on the server, explaining that the person “already has” converted. For an unpaid Explorer that is false.

The ownership pause is already the strong part

Enforced at three independent points, including immediately before the send on freshly resolved evidence. Anyone planning from the prompt alone would assume it needed building. The weakness is the claim, not the stand-down.

Reconciling the three overlapping journey sets found nine scenarios from the original design with no home in the current list. Seven are send-path safety invariants — per-channel suppression, single-winner execution, idempotency, send-time content re-resolution, fallback safety, shadow-mode silence — which get lost precisely when nobody writes down that they were dropped. One is moot while voice is disabled. And one is a real journey the newer list simply omits: high intent plus payment friction, where recovery must outrank selling. It is added back.

The target loop this phase locks

Brand website → Free account → Who is this? → Intelligence → Governor → AI path or Human path → Outcome → Feedback

The canonical entry event is a person successfully creating a free AI account from one of our brand websites. Behaviour before that is acquisition evidence; behaviour after it is journey evidence. A page view is never the start of the lifecycle. That is the sentence the whole reset turns on, and today it is not true in code.
