Status keys: BUILT, PARTIAL, MISSING, DELIBERATE (reason quoted from `tests/systemV2/lib/campusFidelityViews.js` `known` or a code comment), EXTRA (in the build, not the design). ### Part A: Camera and movement

#### A.1 How the design camera works (reference)

| Function (line) | What it does |
|---|---|
| `camLoop` (1730) | Runs every frame. Each frame it eases `cam` toward `camT` by `camEase` (0.06 by default; 0.07 while walking; 0.13 when flying into a room; 0.035 for the zoom-out after the gate). In `follow` mode it re-targets on the avatar (`pos.y-20`). |
| `place` (1729) | Puts a world point at a screen point at scale `s`. `w` is not clamped. |
| `baseView` (1737) | **The recenter.** Shows the whole campus. Desktop: scale `min((W-450)/1010,(H-90)/550,1.6)`, centered on world (530,392) at screen ((420+W)/2,(70+H)/2), which is the area beside the 420px concierge. Phone: scale `min(W/640,1)`, centered on (500,390) at screen y `(mobTop+H*.56)/2`. Sets `camMode='free'`. |
| `focusBuilding` (1741) | Frames one building beside the arrival panel at scale `min(2,(W-440)/480)`. |
| `startFollow` (1745) | The walking camera, at scale `min(1.7,W/760)` (phone `W/470`). |
| `flyIn` (2365) | Arriving: `camEase=.13`, zooms to scale `W/36` (onto the door), then opens the room overlay `#inside` after 360ms. |
| `flyOut` (2373) | Closes the room. If `toQuad`, it calls **`baseView()` after 650ms** at `camEase=.06`. |
| drag (1749-1754) | Pans past 6px and swallows the click at the end of a drag. **Ignored while `traveling`** (1750). |
| wheel (1755) | Zooms around the center, `w` clamped to 260..2200. |
| `routeTo` / `travelTo` (1761/1766) | Path routing: the hub for the upper campus, the avenue for the lower one. While walking it draws a red dashed `#routeLine` and footstep dots (`g-steps`), highlights the target `.bld.hl`, shows the toast "Walking to X..." (or "Driving to X..." with a ride) with **Skip** (`#tt`, `#ttSkip` 1804), and **tucks the concierge** (1780). |
| `finishTravel` / `arrive` (1796/1807) | Snaps the avatar to the door, then `flyIn` (or `focusBuilding` if not animated). It also dims the other buildings and opens `#arr` after 420ms. |

Summary of the design rule: **every way back to "the campus" (closing the card, leaving the room, leaving a page for the map, ending the tour, the Map tab, a resize) eases to the whole-campus `baseView`, beside the concierge.** It uses ease 0.06 per frame (about 1s settle), and 650ms after the room closes. The only target is the whole campus. It never recenters on the user or on a destination. The destination is only framed on arrival (`flyIn`/`focusBuilding`), and the avatar is followed only while walking.

### The most important gaps

1. **Colaberry Live is thin.** No broadcast states (backstage countdown, reconnecting, replay), no timer, no stage or watch-in-place, no reactions, no launch cue or chime on the campus home (the home hides the portal badge that has them), and the live building is the Community Lounge instead of the Classroom. `content_started_at`, `scheduled_start_at`, `editorial_state` and `preview_seconds_remaining` are already in discovery and unused.
2. **The Game Room is not in the campus.** The arcade arrival panel has no break meter, game list or leaderboard. Cabinets in the room are not playable. Games run only on a separate PortalShell page, without the cabinet modal or the game-over sheet. The leaderboard has no avatars or levels, and there is no "your best / rank". Byte Chase and Launch Lab are deliberately different games.
3. **The sponsor (business) experience is mostly missing.** No team stat tiles, no "your team on campus" list, no sponsor person card, no team/seat/invite UI, and no dashboard/seats/digest/demo-day pages in campus chrome. The org APIs (`/api/portal/org/overview|members|invites`) exist and are unused by the campus.
4. **Staff "Needs attention" does not exist.** There is no flagged-learner list, map pins, per-building flags, search insights or one-click view-as. Staff get Management Portal links instead.
5. **The Me panel and Wayfinder are simplified.** No full-body avatar preview, Lv number, icon locker grid, in-panel photo upload or photo prompt, and no unlock or level toasts. The Wayfinder has no modal palette, no `/` or Cmd+K shortcut, no empty-state suggestions or staff, no keyword synonyms, and does not log searches.
