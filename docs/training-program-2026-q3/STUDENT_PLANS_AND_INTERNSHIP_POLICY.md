## 1. Tuition

Two plans. **The difference is the billing term, not the content.**

| Plan | Price | Billing | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Standard | $149 / month | Billed annually, $1,788 for the year | Founding rate. Locks for as long as the member stays a member. |
| Flexible | $199 / month | Billed month to month | Cancel anytime. |

Training, support, projects, mentorship, and community access are **identical on both**. The annual plan is cheaper because it is a year commitment.

Verified against live payment records, not just collateral: a student was charged $199 through PaySimple on 2026-08-10, and the 2026-08-21 auto-renewal notice to existing members states "$199 a month".

### Student-paid third-party costs

Not covered by Colaberry, paid directly to the providers, roughly $30/month on top of tuition:

- Anthropic subscription for Claude Code, about $20/month
- LLM API usage on the student's own key, most projects under $10/month

Source: the founding cohort one-pager. **This was omitted from the 2026-08-25 reply to Dheeraj** and is the most likely cause of a future refund dispute if a lead is not told upfront. See Open Questions.

## 2. Duration

The structured program is **12 weeks**. It does not end there.

- The first three months can be free. **Who qualifies for this is unresolved, see Open Questions.**
- After that, members pay to keep going and can stay as long as they want.
- The course itself, the live classes, and the certification prep material are all part of the membership.

---

## 3. Curriculum

Four intensives, three weeks each. Live classes twice a week, about four hours total: **Architecture Day on Monday, Build Day on Thursday.** Students pick an industry track in week 1 and build their own project in that track across the whole program.

| Weeks | Market name | What they end with |
|---|---|---|
| 1 to 3 | Build Your AI Foundation | Claude Code foundations, a working workspace, reusable Skills, a working Business Workflow Assistant |
| 4 to 6 | Create Your AI Team | A working multi agent system in their own project, plus an Enterprise Prompt Library |
| 7 to 9 | Connect AI To The Real World | Their first production MCP server connected to real business data |
| 10 to 12 | Design AI That Scales | A complete Solution Architecture Package: reliability, governance, architecture documentation |

Anthropic Architect certification prep is included in the membership.

---

## 4. Internship

**The internship is part of what a member gets.** It is not a separate product, and it is not something a student unlocks at week 12. The founding cohort one-pager lists "Full-time internship experience" as a membership inclusion, which is consistent.

### Who pays

| Situation | Fee |
|---|---|
| Already paying Colaberry: current Data Analytics student, or in IPBC | **Waived**, because they are already paying us |
| Everyone else | **Pays the membership** |
| Referred by Ram, or approved by Ali | **Exception, waived** |

### Terms

- **Unpaid.** Interns work on real Colaberry AI projects, normally two at a time, and are held to personal and project KPIs.
- **No fixed end date.** It runs until the person lands a full time role in AI. They give one to two weeks notice once they have an offer.
- **Cannot be employed full time elsewhere.**

The duration and notice terms are not aspirational. They are the written commitment current interns have already made, for example the 2026-08-20 acceptance on the "AI internship" thread.

---

## 5. Enrollment to completion

1. **Enrollment.** Student chooses $149 annual or $199 monthly and pays.
2. **Training.** The 12 week program, live Monday and Thursday.
3. **Projects.** Runs alongside the training, not after it. Students build in their chosen industry track from week 1.
4. **Internship.** Real Colaberry AI project work, unpaid, under the fee rule in section 4.
5. **Completion.** Membership continues for as long as they want it. Certification prep stays available.
