1 · Business Requirements Document

BRD-001 · v1.0 · Owner: Product Sponsor · 2026-07-18

1. Purpose & background

Riverside Grill (8 locations, ~$4M annual revenue) currently takes online orders only through third-party delivery apps that charge 25–30% commission. In 2025 the chain paid an estimated $360K in commissions on ~$1.2M of third-party volume and holds none of that customer data. This initiative delivers a direct, branded online-ordering experience for pickup and in-house delivery.

2. Business objectives

  • Reduce third-party commission cost by moving repeat customers to direct ordering.
  • Own first-party customer and order data for marketing and operations.
  • Increase repeat-order rate through a fast reorder experience.

3. Scope

In scopeWeb + mobile ordering (pickup & own-delivery), menu management, payments, order status, basic order history
Out of scopeLoyalty/points program (Phase 2), replacing the in-store POS, catering & large-group orders, third-party marketplace listings

6. Success criteria

  • ≥ 35% of online orders are direct (not third-party) within 6 months.
  • Commission spend reduced by ≥ $120K in year one.
  • Reorder used on ≥ 25% of returning-customer orders.

7. Assumptions & constraints

  • Assumption: each location already has tablets/printers to receive orders.
  • Constraint: payments go through the existing processor (Stripe); no new processor.
  • Constraint: launch before the November holiday season.

4 · Acceptance Criteria

Given / When / Then

US-2 · Reorder last meal

Given I am signed in and have at least one past order,
When I tap "Reorder" on my last order,
Then my cart is filled with those items at current prices and I reach the payment screen in ≤ 2 taps; out-of-stock items are flagged and removed with a notice.

US-3 · Pay by card

Given a cart with at least one item,
When I submit valid card details,
Then the order is charged, I receive a confirmation number and ETA, and no card number is stored on Riverside systems (tokenized via the processor).

US-5 · 86 an item

Given I am a store manager on the admin view,
When I mark an item out of stock,
Then the item is hidden from that location's menu within 60 seconds and any in-progress carts show it as unavailable at checkout.

Executive Summary

One page

Riverside Grill pays ~$336K/year in third-party commissions and owns none of its customer data. A direct online-ordering experience (pickup + own-delivery, reorder, live menu, card payment) is projected to shift 35% of online volume direct within six months, avoiding ~$120K–$140K in year-one commissions with payback under nine months — while giving Riverside its customer relationship back.

Recommended: proceed to solution architecture (Week 2). Must-haves: live menu + pickup, one-tap reorder, card payment, and manager 86'ing. Deferred: loyalty (Phase 2).

Architect's note. Requirements are specific, testable, prioritized (MoSCoW), and traced. Non-functionals carry real targets (2s menu, 99.5% uptime, PCI SAQ-A, WCAG 2.1 AA). Ready for sign-off.
