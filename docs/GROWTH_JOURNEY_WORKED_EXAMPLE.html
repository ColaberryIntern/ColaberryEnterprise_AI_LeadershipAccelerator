The answer to "how does it choose the content"

Corrected 2026-10-05: this page first said sixteen campaigns and named explorer_high_intent_fast_track at tier 3 as the winner. There are eleven, that key is a test fixture, and the real winner is explorer_enrollment_ready at tier 5.

It doesn't write anything. It picks which of your existing, already-approved campaigns to put the person into.

There are eleven campaigns registered. A human wrote every word in every one of them, and a human approved them. The system's whole job is choosing which envelope this person belongs in — never what the letter says. All eleven, and when each is used →

The only AI anywhere in this pipeline decides who Maria is. Nothing in it composes a sentence.

The example

Maria started a free CPN lesson three weeks ago and stopped after lesson two. This morning she filled in the "next cohort" form and typed a question about start dates.

1

Something happens

a signal

The form submission is recorded. Nothing is interpreted yet — this step only writes down that it happened.

trigger form_submitted
entry site cpn.colaberry.com
what she typed "when does the next data analytics cohort start?"
2

Who is this?

classification · the only AI step

The entry site supplies a default brand. What she actually said can override it. Here they agree, so confidence is high.

brand CPN — from the entry site
programme cpn/learner
path learner_paid_training
confidence 0.820
evidence ["asked about a cohort date", "stalled at lesson 2", "returned after 21 days"]

If confidence had been low — say 0.41 — this row would be flagged requires_human_review and somebody would look at it before anything else happened.

3

What should happen next?

three proposals

Three of the five generators have something to say about Maria. Each one proposes an existing campaign, and attaches a tier and a score.

Who proposesWhich campaignTierScoreBecause
high intentexplorer_enrollment_ready582 asked about a start date, and nothing is blocking her
activation rescueexplorer_activation_restart640 stalled at lesson 2, but has engaged before
nurtureexplorer_weekly_digest910 she exists and is not unsubscribed

Sorted by tier first — lower wins. Tier 5 beats tier 6 beats tier 9, so the score never even gets used here. It would only matter if two proposals shared a tier — which happens for real between explorer_activation_never_started and explorer_activation_restart, both at tier 6.

selected SEND_EMAIL · explorer_enrollment_ready
suppressed explorer_activation_restart — lower priority than tier 5
suppressed explorer_weekly_digest — lower priority than tier 5

The losers are kept. Three weeks from now, "why didn't Maria get the weekly digest?" is answerable from this one row. Nothing is discarded silently.

Note the friction test inside high intent: had Maria also had a blocked payment, friction recovery would have proposed at tier 2 and won — a person fighting a broken thing does not get a cohort email.

4

Is CPN allowed to offer this?

the brand boundary

Checked on every proposal before the sort, and again on the winner afterwards. Deliberately twice.

CPN × learner_paid_training allowed

Had Maria been an AI Flotation lead asking about business training, this is where it would stop — brand_boundary:explicit_deny — and the system would refer her to Colaberry Business rather than sell her something AI Flotation does not offer.

5

Does that campaign actually exist and work?

validation

The campaign is found by its key — never by its name, because names get edited. Five things must all be true:

registered yes — a campaign carries settings.campaign_key
brand-scoped yes — it belongs to CPN, not another brand
approved yes
active yes
has a sequence yes, active

Any one of those failing is a refusal with its own name — campaign_not_approved, campaign_no_sequence, and so on.

6

May we say these things to her?

the content gate

The campaign's assets are checked against what is approved for this exact combination. A deny always beats an allow, however many rules permit it.

brand CPN
offer family learner_paid_training
programme cpn/learner
path learner_paid_training
result approved content exists

This is the step that is blocked in production today. No learner brand has an approved content rule yet, so right now Maria's journey would stop here with content_not_approved — even with every switch turned on.

7

She is enrolled in the campaign

where the words come from

Not "an email is written". Maria is enrolled into the existing sequence, and the sequence's first step is scheduled.

enrollLeadInSequence(lead 4821, sequence 12, campaign 7)
→ the sequence's step-0 email is scheduled

Nobody generated that email. It is the same step-0 email every other person in that campaign receives — written by a human, approved by a human, sitting in the campaign already. The system chose the envelope. The letter was written long before Maria arrived.

8

A receipt is written

the audit trail
action SEND_EMAIL
channel email
campaign explorer_enrollment_ready
status enrolled
reason asked about a start date today

If any step above had refused, this receipt would exist anyway — with the refusal reason instead. Every decision leaves a row, including the ones that decide to do nothing.

So where does the system actually have a choice?

Only in two places. Everything else is a gate that can say no.

ChoiceMade byOut of
Who is this person?classification — the one AI step4 brands × their programmes and paths
Which campaign?a sort by tier, then scorethe 11 registered campaigns
What the email saysnobody — it is already written—

Three things that could still stop this at the very end, after everything above has passed:

A holdout experiment may hold Maria back deliberately, to measure whether this messaging works at all — recorded as chosen_then_blocked, chosen and then intentionally not sent.

A human already talking to her stands the system down.

A pause on CPN, on this programme, on email, or on Maria specifically — and a pause reaches work already in flight.

Nothing in this example has happened. Production is dark: zero classifications, zero decisions, zero enrolments. The names, tiers, campaign keys and refusal reasons are real and read from the code; Maria is not.
